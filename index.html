Held-out validation

Predicting standardised-test composite scores from multimodal brain data

Sibling repo to the TCNL Quanta multimodal brain-age stack (n = 427). Same frozen 80/20 age × sex stratified split (341 train / 86 held-out), same modality blocks — but the targets are seven cognitive and motor composite scores (WAIS Verbal Comprehension Index, three Wechsler memory indices, and three motor composites) instead of chronological age. Chronological age is intentionally excluded from the feature set so the residual prediction reflects only brain + behaviour + sex; the composite targets are already age-normed / age-scaled by their test publishers.

Composite formulations

Each composite target is a summary index that the test publisher computes from underlying subtests. We regressed every target on its plausible component subtests; the fit is exact for the linearly-scored motor composites and approximate for the WAIS / WMS norm indices because the publishers' raw → scaled → norm transformation includes a non-linear lookup-table step.

Composite-score design

Targets were taken from the test-publisher canonical norm / scaled composite indices in the cleaned phenotype file. Candidates that were exact or near-exact linear combinations of the other composites were removed: MEMORY_ST_NORM_ImmMem (R² = 0.996 on AudImm + VisImm), LANGUAGE_ST_SCALED_SUM (R² = 0.982 on RAW_SUM), the raw language sum (not age-normed; superseded by VCI), and LANGUAGE_ST_NORM_PR (R² = 0.873; monotone percentile transform of VCI). After pruning, leave-one-out R² is below 0.42 for every kept composite and the standardized design-matrix condition number is 2.63.

With chronological age excluded from the inputs, behaviour features dominate six of seven targets; DTI is the most informative single block for the language composite. EEG, MRI, deep features, rs-fMRI FC PCA, and the sex covariate contribute marginally in this stacked configuration — the per-block ridge OOFs share most of their general-ability signal.

Composite-prediction deviation (Zhang-corrected)

Per-subject prediction error is corrected for the regression-to-mean bias by subtracting the mean prediction of other-fold subjects within ±4 units of the subject's true composite score (same scheme as the brain-age PAD validation). The corrected deviation is then Pearson-correlated against the 30 BASIC_Q_* questionnaire columns and the 70 unused _ST_ measures.

After Benjamini-Hochberg FDR correction across the 100 features tested per target, each composite has 41–74 FDR-significant correlates. Strong recurring patterns:

  • SF-36 Energy correlates negatively with Motor deviations (worse-than-predicted motor scores covary with lower self-reported energy).
  • Aerobic-rating and waist-hip ratio correlate with Memory deviations (cardiovascular fitness markers track over- and under-prediction).
  • Pegboard and symbol-coding raw sub-scores correlate with Fine Motor and Processing Speed deviations — sibling motor subtests that were not themselves model inputs.
  • Sleep quality (PSQI) and Beck Anxiety / Depression correlate with several Memory deviations.

Full table available in the interactive demo and in reports/deviation_correlations.csv.

Cohort & pipeline

  • Cohort. 427 healthy adults, age 19–80, TCNL Quanta multimodal ageing study.
  • Modalities. behaviour (199) · EEG morphology + PSD (659) · T1-w MRI morphometry (1094) · DTI ROI (90 FA + MD) · deep features (71; SFCN + Pyment) · rs-fMRI Schaefer-400 FC PCA-50 · sex (1). Chronological age is intentionally not a feature.
  • Stack. Per-block RidgeCV out-of-fold predictions → RidgeCV meta-learner. 5-fold inner CV; refit on the full 341 training subjects for the held-out prediction.
  • Split. Inherited from the brain-age held-out repo: 80 / 20 age × sex stratified, frozen ahead of the final run.
